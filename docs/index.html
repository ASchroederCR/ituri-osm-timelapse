Mapping and cases, zone by zone

Each map zooms into the 5.5 km square with the most added buildings in that health zone and draws the real footprints week by week. The roads, rivers and place names come from today's OSM. Next to each map, two charts share one date axis. The upper chart shows buildings in OSM for the whole zone. The lower chart shows cumulative confirmed Ebola cases (solid) and deaths (dotted), from INSP daily SitReps and WHO Disease Outbreak News.
